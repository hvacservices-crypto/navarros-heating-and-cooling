What's Included

A Full HVAC System Check

During a maintenance visit, Navarro's can inspect the major components that affect your system's operation, comfort, and reliability.

  • Inspect thermostat operation
  • Inspect filters and airflow
  • Check temperature split
  • Inspect electrical components
  • Check contactors and capacitors
  • Inspect blower operation
  • Inspect outdoor condenser
  • Clean condenser coil when applicable
  • Inspect refrigerant system operation
  • Check visible drain and condensate components

Built for Las Vegas Homes

Cooling systems in Las Vegas can run hard for months at a time. Regular inspections can help prepare your equipment for peak summer temperatures.

  • Prepare the system before summer
  • Catch electrical problems early
  • Identify dirty coils and airflow restrictions
  • Check for abnormal system operation
  • Help reduce unexpected breakdowns

Home Comfort Plan FAQ

How many maintenance visits are included?

The current mockup includes two seasonal HVAC maintenance visits per year. Final plan terms can be adjusted before launch.

Are repairs included?

Repairs are not automatically included in the plan. The plan can offer discounted repair pricing for members.

Are filters included?

Filter inspection is included in this mockup. Whether filter replacement is included can be decided when final pricing and plan terms are created.

Can I join if my HVAC system is older?

The plan can be offered for many existing systems, although system condition may be documented during the first maintenance visit.

How much does the plan cost?

Pricing has not been finalized yet. The website currently shows "Coming Soon" so Navarro's can choose the final price before launch.
